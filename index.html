WELCOME TO MOTI MAHAL DELUX

A Perfect Blend of Taste, Tradition & Elegance

Step into a world of refined flavours and warm hospitality at Moti Mahal Delux, Siwan's premier multicuisine destination. Our restaurant brings together the rich heritage of North Indian and Mughlai cuisine, the bold aromas of Chinese dishes, and the smoky charm of Tandoori specialities.

With premium interiors, comfortable seating, and a fine-dining ambiance, we are the ideal setting for family dinners, birthday celebrations, and special gatherings. Every visit is crafted to be a memorable dining experience.
